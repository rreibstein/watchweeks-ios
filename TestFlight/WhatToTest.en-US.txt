WatchWeeks 1.0.3 — Safer saved-guide loading

This update validates cached public guides before displaying them and supports recovery on refresh while preserving saved ratings, Seen state and valid older guides. It addresses a reproduced malformed-cache crash risk; the reported iOS 27.3 beta crash is not yet confirmed fixed.

Install as an update over your existing app. Open Home, a guide, New releases and My list; verify your saved ratings and Seen state. Close and reopen the app. If a guide was already cached, check it and My list in Airplane Mode, then reconnect and refresh.

If possible, test opening on iOS 27.3 beta. Report the exact version/build and whether Home appears before any unexpected closure. Do not delete/reinstall to test.

No new features, permissions, analytics or push enrollment. No App Review submission or publication is automatic from this upload.
