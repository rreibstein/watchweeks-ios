# WatchWeeks iPhone app — rapid content update privacy policy

This policy covers the WatchWeeks rapid content update. WatchWeeks is operated
by Reed Reibstein in the United States. Privacy contact: reed.reibstein@gmail.com.

## Your saved information

Ratings, shortlist, seen marks and other visit selections stay in the app's local
storage. Previously loaded guides and event programs are cached for offline use.
The app does not upload these selections or synchronize with website accounts.
iOS backups may include local app storage under your Apple settings. This update
does not erase prior selections, caches or consent preferences.

## Public content requests

The app retrieves public guides, photos and the published IAMWATCH program over
HTTPS. WatchWeeks and its hosting/image providers receive request information,
including an IP address and requested resource, to deliver and protect content.
Provider logs may be kept for operational, security or legal purposes; retention
depends on the provider and record. Contact us for access/deletion questions.
Provider bot-protection cookies may support these content requests.

The native program is extracted from the public WatchWeeks program page without
running its JavaScript or embedding a web browser. Website analytics scripts do
not run as part of that native program display.

## No native product analytics or push enrollment in this build

This rapid build has no configured PostHog client, analytics consent prompt, APNs
registration or push subscription. It creates no analytics identifier and does
not transmit a queue left by an earlier TestFlight build. Apple operates separate
App Store/TestFlight analytics and beta diagnostics under Apple's own terms.
Deferred analytics and push source is maintained separately; a later enabled
release will provide its own accurate disclosures and choices.

## Sharing and links

You choose recipients when using iOS sharing. External links open through iOS;
the destination's own privacy policy applies. Website accounts/analytics are
separate features, described at https://watchweeks.com/privacy.

## Retention and choices

Saved visit data stays on-device until removed by you or app deletion. This update
preserves prior data. Do not delete the app merely to test an update. Manage iOS
backups using Apple's settings. Requests already served and provider logs may
remain for their operational, security or legal purposes. Contact the address
above for privacy questions and access/correction/deletion requests.
